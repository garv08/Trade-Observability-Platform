How to read this

CAGR ÷ |Max DD| (the MAR ratio) rewards growth earned with shallow drawdowns. 30% CAGR with a −15% worst drawdown scores 2.0; 30% with −60% scores 0.5. Drawdowns smaller than 5% count as 5%, so a rule that barely traded can't top the table.

PSR / DSR: with ~92 strategies tried per script, some Sharpes are high by luck. PSR is the chance the true Sharpe is above zero; DSR (deflated Sharpe) is the chance it beats the best Sharpe expected from that many lucky tries. Set Min deflated Sharpe to 95 to keep only results unlikely to be luck. Both assume normal daily returns, so treat them as optimistic.

Each row is one long/flat strategy with its default settings on one script, with ₹10 lakh, 5 bps cost per side, on all stored daily history (about 5 years; BSE indices start in April 2025). Click a row to open it on the chart.

Scripts with thin trading (bars on under half the weekdays) or impossible one-day moves (above +100% or below −50% after split adjustment) are skipped, as are Dividend Points and VIX.

With 92 strategies tried on 5,000+ scripts, some results are luck. Prefer strategies that pass on many scripts, enough trades, and full history. Research only, not investment advice.
